# Least Count

Least Count uses two physical standard decks (104 uniquely identified cards). Cards match by rank only; suits are visual. A player begins with five cards and, on a turn, drops a same-rank group onto the top discard. A matching drop avoids the draw; otherwise the player draws exactly one card. The opening discard may be met by any drop.

The top discard may be picked only after a drop, and older discards cannot be selected. A blocked Joker can never be picked or returned to the draw pile. Friends games select one Joker rank across both decks; those cards score zero. System games do not use Jokers.

Players may call **Least Count** on their turn. The server calculates scores automatically. A uniquely lowest caller (score 0–5) scores zero; if another player is uniquely lower, the caller scores 50. A tied lowest score keeps the round alive. Lowest cumulative score wins the selected number of rounds.
